How reserving a kitten works

  1. Join the waitlist

    Send us a quick email with your name and what you're hoping for.

  2. Hear about litters

    We email everyone on the list when a litter is born, with photos.

  3. Reserve your kitten

    Meet the kittens on video or in person, then choose. Deposit details coming soon.

  4. Bring them home

    Kittens go home at around 12 to 14 weeks, once they're ready.

What comes home with your kitten

  • A veterinary health check and records of any vaccinations
  • A small bag of the food they're used to eating
  • A blanket that smells like mom and their littermates
  • Our phone number, for any question, any time
