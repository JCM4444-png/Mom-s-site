About Opal

Opal is long, lean and still growing into her ears, which already have proper lynx tufts on the tips. Her coat is mostly white with a black cap, a black saddle and a big black plume of a tail.

She's playful and nosy in the best way. Opal follows us from room to room, supervises anything that happens on the kitchen counter, and then stretches out on the mantel to watch the yard. When she's relaxed she rolls onto her back and waits for someone to notice.

Her best friend is Iris. The two of them share window spots, nap pressed together, and take turns chasing each other down the hall in the evenings.
