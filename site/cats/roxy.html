About Roxy

Roxy is the fluffiest cat in the house. She has a full lion's ruff, a patchwork coat of orange, black and white, and a tail so thick she can wrap it all the way around herself when she curls up.

She's easygoing and affectionate, and very little bothers her. Roxy has claimed the printer by the window as her personal lookout, and she will happily stay there all afternoon, one paw dangling over the edge.

In the evenings she gets her second wind and turns playful, especially with a wand toy or a willing friend on the hallway rug.
