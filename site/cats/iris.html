About Iris

Iris has a soft gray tabby coat with a white chest, white paws and a white blaze down her nose. Her eyes are wide and round, which gives her a permanently thoughtful look.

She's quieter than Opal and a little more reserved with new people. Give her a few minutes and she'll sit close by, then closer, and eventually she'll be leaning on you. She loves the front window, especially on rainy afternoons when there's plenty to watch.

Iris and Opal are almost always together. If you find one of them, the other is usually within a few feet.
