Meet the three cats who started it all

November 4, 2026

Welcome to the DB Cattery blog. Before we post litter news and kitten updates, we want you to meet the three cats behind it all. They're the moms of every kitten we'll raise, and they run the house.

Roxy

Roxy is our calico and the most relaxed cat we've ever known. She has a huge ruff, a tail like a feather duster and a habit of falling asleep upside down in whatever patch of sun she can find.

Opal

Opal is our black and white explorer. She has tall, tufted ears and an opinion about everything. Opal follows us around the house and inspects every bag that comes through the door.

Iris

Iris is our gray tabby and the quiet one. She watches first and joins in later. She and Opal are almost never apart, and they share every window seat in the house.

What's next

We're planning our first litters now. Each cat has her own page with more photos and videos, and we'll post here as soon as there's kitten news. If you'd like to hear first, join the waitlist.
