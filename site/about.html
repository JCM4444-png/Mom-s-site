About us

A family, a beach house, and three very large cats.

How we started

We fell for Maine Coons the way most people do: one big, chatty, dog-like cat at a time. Roxy came first, then Opal and Iris joined the family, and before long our house in Daytona Beach was arranged around who gets which window.

DB Cattery grew out of that. We wanted to raise kittens the same way our own cats live: indoors, in the middle of family life, handled every day and used to people from the start.

Why Maine Coons

Maine Coons are one of the largest domestic cat breeds. They're known for tufted ears, thick ruffs, long plumed tails and big personalities to match.

Most are gentle, patient and social. They tend to follow their people from room to room, get along well with children and other pets, and talk in chirps and trills rather than loud meows.

How we raise our kittens

We keep the cattery small on purpose, so every kitten gets real time and attention.

  • Our cats are pets first. They live in the house with us, not in cages or a separate building.
  • Kittens stay with their mom until they're old enough to go home, usually around 12 to 14 weeks.
  • Every kitten is checked by our veterinarian before going to a new family.
  • We'll share registration and health testing details for each cat on her profile page.
  • We're always reachable after adoption, whether it's a question in week one or year five.
